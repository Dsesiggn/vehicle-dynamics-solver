02 What this means

In a planar turn with zero lateral tire slip, wheel-normal lines meet at a common center on the unsteered rear axle extension.

cot(δoutside) − cot(δinside) = T / L

The relation above uses positive angle magnitudes, wheelbase L, and front track T. The workbench displays signed headings: positive is rightward; negative is leftward.

This benchmark excludes real steering-linkage geometry, suspension travel, compliance, tire slip and tire forces. Software checks do not establish physical validation for a vehicle.
